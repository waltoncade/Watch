About

A hobby watch, in the open

Watch is Cade’s custom open-hardware PCB smartwatch. It is an informal project for now. This site is a placeholder home until the project is hosted officially.

The project

A board you can wear

The thing being built is a round four-layer circuit board, 1.6 mm thick, meant to be a watch. It is drawn in KiCad, fabricated and assembled at JLCPCB on stackup JLC04161H-7628, and the firmware is a PlatformIO project.

The published parts are a Bosch BMA400 accelerometer, an OSRAM SFH 5711 light sensor, a 110 mAh coin cell charged by an MCP73831, an onboard antenna, and status LEDs. The hardware page is the full list. The build log is where the bench notes go.
