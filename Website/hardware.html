Known hardware

The board, the sensors, the cell

The board is round, four layers, 1.6 mm thick, on JLCPCB’s JLC04161H-7628 stackup. It is designed in KiCad and both fabricated and assembled at JLCPCB.

Motion is a Bosch BMA400, an ultra-low-power 3-axis accelerometer. It talks I2C. Its interrupt is wired to the MCU, for motion and for step/wake detection. The MCU part number is not in these notes.

Ambient light is an OSRAM SFH 5711, used for auto-brightness.

Power is a 3.7 V, 110 mAh Li-ion coin cell, Cornell Dubilier / Illinois Capacitor RJD2430C1. An MCP73831 charges that cell at 50 mA / 4.2 V.

Wireless is an onboard antenna fed by a 50-ohm coplanar RF trace. Which radio sits on that feed is not written down here.

Status LEDs are fitted. Firmware is built with PlatformIO.
