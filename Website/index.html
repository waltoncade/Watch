01 — On the board

Only what the notes name

Each item below is taken from the hardware that is actually specified.

01

Motion

Bosch BMA400, an ultra-low-power 3-axis accelerometer. It sits on I2C, and its interrupt goes to the MCU, for motion and step/wake detection.

02

Auto-brightness

OSRAM SFH 5711 ambient light sensor, used to set brightness from the room.

03

Coin cell

3.7 V, 110 mAh Li-ion coin cell, Cornell Dubilier / Illinois Capacitor RJD2430C1. An MCP73831 charges it at 50 mA / 4.2 V.

04

Wireless

Onboard antenna with a 50-ohm coplanar RF feed. The radio protocol is not written down yet.

05

Status LEDs

Indicators on the board for bring-up and state. Colors are not specified.

06

Open board

Round 4-layer PCB, 1.6 mm, JLCPCB stackup JLC04161H-7628. Designed in KiCad. Fabricated and assembled at JLCPCB. Firmware is built with PlatformIO.
