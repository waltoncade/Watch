What’s inside

    The dial

    A circle of light

    Around the dial sits a ring of twenty-four small lights, one for every hour of the day. They frame the hands and give the face a quiet glow of its own.

    Awareness

    Made to notice

    Walton one can sense the light around it and the movement of your wrist. How it puts that to use is still being refined. More to share soon.

    Power

    Charged, not replaced

    Inside is a rechargeable battery. Top it up and carry on, with no tiny batteries to hunt down. Battery life will be shared once testing is complete.
